Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

ToggyBot is a non-commercial Discord moderation bot used exclusively in Toggy's Discord server.

Contact: definitelyhuman@anthem-syndicate.com

What data is processed?

ToggyBot may process Discord user IDs, usernames, relevant message content, and timestamps to provide its moderation features. It stores:

  • Moderation notes and warnings created by authorized moderators, including their reasons and relevant details.
  • Transcripts of moderation or appeal cases, which are posted in a restricted #transcripts channel on Discord.

Ordinary server messages may be processed temporarily for moderation assistance but are not kept as a general message archive by ToggyBot.

Why is it processed?

This data is used to maintain server safety, document moderation decisions, and handle appeals. Where applicable, processing is based on legitimate interests in effective community moderation (GDPR Article 6(1)(f)), balanced against members' rights and interests.

Who can access it?

Stored moderation records are available to authorized server moderators. Transcripts posted to Discord are also processed by Discord under its own privacy terms. Message content used for automated moderation assistance is processed on privately operated infrastructure, without sending it to third-party AI inference providers.

How long is it kept?

Records are retained only while needed for moderation or appeals and are reviewed or removed when no longer necessary. Discord hosted transcript copies are subject to deletion and retention actions on Discord as well.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal data associated with you, and object to processing, by contacting definitelyhuman@anthem-syndicate.com. Requests are assessed under applicable law and Discord's developer requirements. You may also complain to a relevant data protection supervisory authority.

ToggyBot does not make final moderation decisions automatically. Authorized moderators remain responsible for moderation actions.

Changes

This policy may be updated when ToggyBot's features or data practices change. The date above will reflect the latest revision.

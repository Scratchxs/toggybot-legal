# ToggyBot Terms of Service

**Last updated:** October 16th, 2026

ToggyBot is a free, non-commercial moderation bot developed for use exclusively in Toggy's Discord server.

## Use

ToggyBot helps authorized moderators manage notes, warnings, appeals, and other moderation tasks. Its use is subject to Discord's Terms of Service and the server's rules.

## Moderation

Moderation decisions are made by the server's moderators. ToggyBot may assist with organizing information, but its automated output can be incomplete or inaccurate. The server's moderation team remains responsible for any actions taken.

## Availability

ToggyBot is provided as available. Features may change, be unavailable, or be discontinued without advance notice. Nothing in these terms limits rights that cannot legally be excluded.
